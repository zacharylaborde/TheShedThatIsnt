The shed that isn’t

There’s an ordinary garden shed in this field, with a door at the front and another at the back.

Behind each door is a short hallway that feels perfectly normal to walk through. On the map, though, each hallway is a funnel, and as you walk it, the metric e2φ(dx² + dy² + dz²) smoothly grows you into a hall twenty times bigger than the shed, or shrinks you back out.

A dark-framed door in the hall’s left wall is a shortcut of a different kind: it’s glued straight to a garden wall in the field.

Left thumb walks. Right thumb looks. On a computer, use WASD and drag with the mouse.
